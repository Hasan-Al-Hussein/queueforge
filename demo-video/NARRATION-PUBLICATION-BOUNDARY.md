# Narration publication boundary

Recorded 2026-09-26. The QueueForge editable demo source identifies narration generated
through the external Edge TTS service with voice `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`.
The available source and production records establish provenance, but they do not include
terms or written permission establishing redistribution rights for the generated voice
output.

For that reason, this public preservation branch excludes exactly the ten generated MP3
files and the narrated MP4 listed below. The original local files were not modified or
deleted. The archived `QueueForgeVideo.tsx` composition still references the local MP3s
and therefore cannot render unchanged until cleared replacement audio is supplied.

Fourteen retained browser clips have no audio streams. Those clips, the captured
screenshots, caption and timing files, recording scripts, editable Remotion scenes,
licensed fonts, engineering source, and historical receipts remain public evidence.
